## Analytics & Tracking

This website does not use any analytic or tracking technologies to collect information about what you read or click on.

If your browser blocks something on a page because of this website's security rules, or notices the website using a browser feature that's being phased out, it may send this website a short report about it. Only the page's address and what happened are logged, never your IP address or browser details, and it's only used to find and fix problems.

## Use of Cookies

This website does not use cookies.

If you choose a colour scheme (Light or Dark), your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so it's remembered between pages and visits. It's just the word "light" or "dark", it stays on your device, and it's never sent to this website. Choosing "System" removes it.

### Third party site disclaimer

Please note that external sites linked to from our website may use cookies. The owners of this website are not responsible for this. You need to check with these external sites what information is being collected using cookies.

## Embedded Videos

Some pages include videos hosted on YouTube. These are click-to-load: until you press play, you only see a preview image that's hosted on this website, and nothing is requested from YouTube.

When you press play, the video loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced service (youtube-nocookie.com). From that point YouTube (Google) receives information such as your IP address, and may store data on your device, as described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). If you'd rather not, each video also has a link to watch it on YouTube instead, or you can simply not play it.
