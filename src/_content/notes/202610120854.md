I’m approaching the point where I select music services based on whether or not I can block artists. I still don’t understand why I can’t mute entire genres when I’m browsing a video streaming service. All this math, all this time, all this work, all this capital, all of it dedicated to delivering the most predictable, boring, average thing. We’ll take all the data you’ve given us, and we’ll press it down into the content equivalent of beige, flavorless paste.

And suddenly, the “artificial intelligence” industry makes a lot more sense.
