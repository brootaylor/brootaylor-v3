It's such a shame that people are getting fed up with the state of the web industry. I feel the same way much of the time.

But... it didn't have to be like this. It doesn't have to be like this.

Like [Jeremy Keith says](https://adactio.com/journal/22778#:~:text=My%20issue%20with,local%20language%20models.):

> My issue with large language models has never been the actual technology, which is genuinely fascinating. My issue is with the power dynamics, of being robbed of agency, of the over-inflated hype and criti-hype being used to convince us of an inevitable future where a small group of very rich men are masters over our lives.
>
> I’d love to see the technology decoupled from the current depressing narrative. I’d love to see a thousand homemade software flowers bloom from open local language models.
